All You Wonder Studio

A small studio app for one YouTube channel

All You Wonder Studio is a private desktop tool that produces short educational videos for the YouTube channel All You Wonder (@alluwonder) and uploads them to that channel after the channel owner approves each one.

What it does

Makes videos locally

Original scripts, AI-generated illustrations and synthetic narration are produced on the owner's own computer.

Checks every export

Automated checks cover resolution, length, loudness, captions and sources. The owner then reviews each video.

Uploads to one channel

Approved videos are uploaded only to the owner's channel through the YouTube Data API, starting as private.

Labels AI content

Every video is marked as containing synthetic media. Fiction is labelled as an original story.

The app has a single user, the channel owner. It has no sign-up, no public accounts and no access to anyone else's channel or data.
