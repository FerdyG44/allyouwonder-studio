All You Wonder Studio

A small studio app for one YouTube channel

All You Wonder Studio is a private desktop tool that produces short educational videos for the YouTube channel All You Wonder (@alluwonder) and uploads them to that channel after the channel owner approves each one.

Effective 26 October 2026 · Applies to All You Wonder Studio

About the app

All You Wonder Studio is a private desktop application operated by Ferdi Gülseren. It makes videos for the All You Wonder YouTube channel and uploads them to that channel after the channel owner approves each one. It is not offered to the public and has no user accounts other than the channel owner.

YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. Use of the app is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of personal data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. The app's own data practices are described in the All You Wonder Studio Privacy Policy.

What the app does with your YouTube account

  • It requests only the youtube.upload permission.
  • It uploads only videos that the channel owner has reviewed and approved in the app.
  • Every upload starts as private. Public release is set by the channel owner.
  • It does not delete, edit or comment on existing videos, and it does not access other channels.

Content

Videos are produced from original scripts with AI-generated visuals and synthetic narration. Each upload is marked as containing synthetic media, factual videos list their sources, and fictional videos are labelled as original stories. The channel owner is responsible for the videos published on the channel and for complying with YouTube's Community Guidelines.

Revoking access

The channel owner can revoke the app's access at any time on the Google Account permissions page. Data deletion steps are listed in the Privacy Policy.
